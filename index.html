01 / THE IDEA

Don't make people
sort the future.

Conventional segregation depends heavily on repetitive manual work. INFINITY SORT explores a different model: let machines perform the visual recognition and repetitive pick-and-place work while people focus on supervision, maintenance and higher-value tasks.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

It doesn't just detect.
It acts.

Many systems stop after identifying an object. Our platform connects perception to physical action — classification becomes a robotic decision, and the decision becomes movement.

SEE → DECIDE → MOVE One continuous perception-to-action loop.

02 / THE SYSTEM

Six steps.
One intelligent loop.

01

Capture

Camera observes the conveyor workspace.

02

Understand

AI analyses the visible object's characteristics.

03

Locate

The target position is estimated for manipulation.

04

Decide

The controller selects the appropriate action.

05

Move

Servo control translates the decision into motion.

06

Sort

The object is transferred to its target category.
